Resources

General educational information about menstrual health. This content is not a diagnosis.

Understanding the Menstrual Cycle

A menstrual cycle is counted from the first day of one period to the first day of the next period. Cycle timing and bleeding patterns can vary from person to person and from cycle to cycle.

Tracking Symptoms

Recording the date, symptom, intensity, and impact on daily activities can help you describe your experience during a healthcare appointment. A record is not a diagnosis by itself.

When to seek medical advice

Consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional if symptoms are worrying, persistent, worsening, interfere with daily life, or feel unusual for you. Seek urgent medical care for severe or sudden symptoms.
